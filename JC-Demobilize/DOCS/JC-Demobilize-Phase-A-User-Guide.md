# Updating Your Logon Experience

*A short guide for using this on your company-managed Mac.*

---

## What this does

We're updating how you sign in to your Mac. The change makes password resets more consistent and streamlined going forward, and it removes some older background settings on your Mac that you don't need anymore. To finalize the change on your machine, you'll be asked to log out and back in once. After that, you're set.

---

## What you'll see

About every two hours while the update is pending, a small window will appear in the middle of your screen titled **Quick Update to Your Logon Experience**. It explains the change and offers two buttons:

- **Log Out Now** — logs you out within 60 seconds. You'll get one more brief countdown window so you can finish saving anything.
- **Remind Me Later** — closes the window. It'll pop up again at the next reminder.

After a few reminders, the window will start showing how many days you have left before the logout happens automatically. If you keep deferring past that point, you'll see a different window with a **5-minute countdown**, and your Mac will log you out at the end of it — so please save your work as soon as you see it.

When you log back in, you might also see a small follow-up window titled **SecureToken Required** asking for your account password. If it appears, enter the same password you just signed in with — this enables a small security feature on your account so things like password changes work correctly.

Once the update finishes, you'll see a brief **Account Conversion Complete** confirmation, and the reminders stop.

---

## If something doesn't work

Try these quick things first:

1. **Save your work and click Log Out Now** — most of the time, that's all this needs. After you log back in, the reminders should stop within an hour.
2. **Make sure you log in at the cloud-logo screen** — when you log back in, look for your organization's login window with the cloud-logo background. If you see the standard macOS login window instead, click your username and try again — the cloud-logo screen is the one that finishes the update.
3. **If a password prompt appears, use your current account password** — the **SecureToken Required** window asks for the password you sign in with daily. Three tries are allowed.
4. **If your Mac keeps logging you out repeatedly** — that means the update isn't finishing for some reason. Contact the Service Desk so they can take a closer look.
