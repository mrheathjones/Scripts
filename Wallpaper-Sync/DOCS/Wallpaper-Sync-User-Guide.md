# Matching Login Background

*A short guide for your company-managed Mac.*

---

## What this does

Your Mac keeps the login screen background matched to your desktop wallpaper. Whenever you change your
wallpaper, the login screen you see when you sign in updates to match — automatically, with nothing for
you to set up.

---

## What you'll see

Nothing while it happens — it runs quietly in the background. The only thing you'll notice is that the
next time you sign in, the login screen background matches the wallpaper you last chose.

---

## If something doesn't work

Try these quick things first:

1. **Restart your Mac and wait a few minutes** — this lets the background updater start fresh.
2. **Change your wallpaper again** — pick your wallpaper once more in System Settings, then sign out and back in to check.
